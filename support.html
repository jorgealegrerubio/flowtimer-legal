Frequently asked questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. There is no account and no cloud sync.

I don't get a notification when a task ends. Check that notifications are enabled in Settings and in your system settings. On Android, battery optimization can delay them.

Why does the timer keep counting after zero? FlowTimer measures the real time a task takes. Tap Complete when you finish, or turn on automatic advance in Settings.
