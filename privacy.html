Privacy policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

FlowTimer is a routine timer app for Android and iOS, developed by Jorge Alegre Rubio. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Your routines stay on your device

Routines, tasks, session history and statistics are stored only in a local database on your phone. FlowTimer has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We never receive, sell or share this data. It is not used for advertising.

Advertising

FlowTimer is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, Google may collect or receive:

  • device and advertising identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location;
  • device and app information;
  • ad interaction data.

Ad requests never include your routine or task names or your productivity data. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent

Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, FlowTimer asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. The app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps (App Tracking Transparency on iOS), and it does not use fingerprinting.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your device to tell you when a task's time is up. They are created locally and never leave your phone.

Children

FlowTimer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual sessions and routines in the app. To delete everything, uninstall FlowTimer. Nothing remains on any server, because nothing was ever sent to one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
